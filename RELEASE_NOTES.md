### Hello!
Welcome to the very first ALPHA pre-release of plugsalot. I am so excited about this! I am so busy and have very little time to actually produce music. But I am a songwriter and as I am sure many muso's out there, I just want to get my music out of my brain and heart and into the world. So very often I will have an hour or two if I am lucky, in the week to record something. 

**Plugins suck**
Plugins are a headache! I just recently wrote a long article which I will put on my website soon ([www.adinwalls.com](https://www.adinwalls.com)) helping Linux music producers get set up with Mixbus which is a less popular but really cool DAW... that article is a 14 page Google Doc! It's just so much headroom to keep all that info in my mind when I have actual work to do... and so I made Plugsalot.

**Amazing plugins that I love**
I don't ACTUALLY think plugins suck. Actually, I LOVE plugins, so this Github is a dream come true for me. There are so many plugins that I love and developers who are unsung heroes of the open-source plugin world. So I have amalgamated them into one easy to use interface. You can check the main page of this Github Repo for full credits to everyone, but devs like Chris Johnson https://www.airwindows.com/ who works tirelessly on his incredible Airwindows library of plugins; people like this have taken us from relying on companies like Waves Audio (no shade!) and needing to use Windows or Mac to run their management software, to being able to create pro sound on Linux or any OS we want, without being tied to subscriptions etc.

**What Plugsalot does**
I have compiled alot of my favourite and highly recommended open source plugins into the Plugsalot registry. When you first use it you need to add a free Gemini AI (Google AI Studio) API key or your Claude.ai API key (paid version). This will give Plugsalot a "brain". Once you tell Plugsalot what you are recording via the dropdown choices and the Chat to Plugsalot section, it will enable the plugins you need - all with notes on what plugin it is, what it's doing and what open source license it's running on. It even does guitar tones with the guitarix suite of plugins https://guitarix.org/ (still testing - may not work on Windows and Mac well). I have left the system open to AI models so that as LLM's get smarter or perhaps you decide to pay for a better AI model, Plugsalot will get better too.
